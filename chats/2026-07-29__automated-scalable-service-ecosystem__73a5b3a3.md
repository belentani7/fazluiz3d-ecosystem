# 1. Resumen ejecutivo: qué sistema conviene construir

La forma empresarial más amplia, automatizada y escalable no es “una app” ni “una tienda online”. Es una **plataforma de mercado de servicios asistida por agentes**, con tres motores simultáneos:

1. **Motor de inteligencia de demanda**: detecta lagunas de oferta/demanda en servicios locales, digitales y B2B.
2. **Motor de producción/activación de servicios**: crea servicios digitales automatizables o activa proveedores humanos verificados.
3. **Motor de intermediación y cobro**: conecta cliente-proveedor, gestiona presupuesto, pago, entrega, reseñas, disputas y comisión.

Nombre operativo sugerido: **SAIPS** — *Sistema Autónomo de Intermediación y Producción de Servicios*.

**Tesis central.**  
El diseño óptimo es una **plataforma modular, API-first, event-driven, multiagente, con pagos regulados, cumplimiento automatizado y gobierno humano en decisiones de alto riesgo**. No existe hoy un sistema 100 % “autoactualizado, blindado y rentable” sin supervisión; lo que sí existe es una automatización muy alta con *human-on-the-loop*: la máquina propone, ejecuta y mide; el humano aprueba cambios legales, financieros, de seguridad y de reputación.

**Flujo núcleo.**

> Señal de demanda → hipótesis de servicio → oferta generada o proveedor activado → matching → presupuesto/contrato → pago en escrow → entrega → reseña/soporte → liquidación con comisión → aprendizaje automático → nueva optimización.

**Modelo de ingresos recomendado.**

- Comisión por transacción: 10–25 % en servicios locales; 5–15 % en B2B alto ticket; 20–70 % en productos digitales propios.
- Suscripción para proveedores: herramientas, visibilidad, seguro, facturación, CRM.
- Suscripción para clientes B2B: paquetes de servicios recurrentes.
- Servicios financieros auxiliares: anticipos, seguros, facturación, *embedded finance*, con partner regulado.

**Mayor escalabilidad posible.**  
La escalabilidad máxima viene de: **coste marginal bajo**, **efectos de red**, **datos propios**, **automatización de operaciones**, **expansión por ciudades/categorías** y **APIs para que terceros construyan encima**. Pero la liquidez de mercado se consigue primero en un nicho geográfico o vertical denso, por ejemplo Pubilla Cases / L’Hospitalet / Barcelona metropolitana.

# 3. Triangulación de perspectivas

## 3.1 Perspectiva científica/económica

La empresa existe porque reduce costes de transacción: buscar, negociar, contratar, verificar y hacer cumplir acuerdos. Esto lo explicó **Ronald Coase** en *The Nature of the Firm* (1937) y lo desarrolló **Oliver Williamson** en *The Economic Institutions of Capitalism* (1985). Tu sistema debe minimizar cinco fricciones:

1. **Búsqueda**: encontrar cliente/proveedor.
2. **Información**: saber quién es fiable.
3. **Contratación**: presupuesto, términos, consentimiento.
4. **Pago**: cobrar, retener, liquidar.
5. **Cumplimiento**: entrega, garantía, disputa.

En mercados de dos lados, la literatura de plataformas —**Rochet y Tirole, 2003**; **Parker, Van Alstyne y Choudary, 2016**; **Cusumano, Gawer y Yoffie, 2019**— muestra que el valor no está solo en el producto, sino en la liquidez: cuantos más compradores y vendedores, mejor matching, más datos y más barrera de entrada.

En IA, el sistema se apoya en:

- **Agentes autónomos**: Wooldridge, *An Introduction to MultiAgent Systems* (2009); Russell y Norvig, *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* (4.ª ed., 2021).
- **Aprendizaje por refuerzo y optimización**: Sutton y Barto, *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction* (2.ª ed., 2018).
- **Experimentación causal**: Kohavi, Tang y Xu, *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments* (2020); Angrist y Pischke, *Mostly Harmless Econometrics* (2009); Imbens y Rubin, *Causal Inference* (2015).
- **Economía de datos**: Einav y Levin, “Economics of Big Data” (2014); Goldfarb y Tucker, *Digital Economics* (2019).

**Certeza alta:** los efectos de red, la confianza y la liquidez son determinantes.  
**Certeza media:** la IA puede automatizar gran parte del descubrimiento, producción y atención.  
**Certeza baja:** que un sistema se autooptimice completamente sin fallos legales, técnicos o reputacionales.

## 3.2 Perspectiva práctica/operativa

La forma más realista de empezar no es “construir el sistema mundial”, sino un **MVP concierge** en un nicho:

- 1 barrio: Pubilla Cases / L’Hospitalet.
- 1–3 categorías: soporte digital a mayores, reparaciones del hogar, cuidado por horas, limpieza, gestoría básica, marketing para comercios.
- 10–30 proveedores verificados.
- 100–500 clientes potenciales.
- Comisión inicial 10–20 %.
- Operaciones semiautomáticas con WhatsApp, calendario, pagos, CRM y plantillas.

Después se automatiza:

- Lead scoring.
- Presupuestos.
- Verificación de proveedores.
- Pagos escrow.
- Facturación.
- Reseñas.
- Disputas.
- Recomendación.
- Precios dinámicos.
- Producción de servicios digitales.

Metodologías relevantes: **Lean Startup** de Eric Ries (2011); **Customer Development** de Steve Blank (2013); **Continuous Discovery Habits** de Teresa Torres (2021); **Continuous Delivery** de Humble y Farley (2010); **Accelerate** de Forsgren, Humble y Kim (2018).

**Certeza alta:** sin validación de demanda y liquidez inicial, la automatización amplifica errores.  
**Certeza alta:** los pagos y la confianza deben estar resueltos desde el día 1.  
**Certeza media:** la automatización total de servicios físicos es limitada; la de servicios digitales es mucho mayor.

## 3.3 Perspectiva ético-histórica

Históricamente, las plataformas escalan porque externalizan costes y riesgos: infraestructura, mano de obra, inventario, atención. Eso genera eficiencia, pero también conflictos laborales, concentración de poder y vigilancia.

Referentes críticos:

- **Shoshana Zuboff**, *The Age of Surveillance Capitalism* (2019): riesgo de extraer datos conductuales y manipular usuarios.
- **Trebor Scholz**, *Platform Cooperativism* (2016): alternativa de gobernanza y reparto de valor.
- **David Autor**, “Why Are There Still So Many Jobs?” (2015): la automatización sustituye tareas, no siempre empleos completos.
- Historia de plataformas: Uber, Airbnb, Deliveroo, Glovo, Amazon, App Store: innovación rápida seguida de litigios, regulación y ajustes laborales/fiscales.

**Riesgos éticos principales:**

1. Precarización de proveedores.
2. Sesgo algorítmico en ranking, precios o selección.
3. Opacidad de comisiones.
4. Uso excesivo de datos personales.
5. Desplazamiento de comercio local.
6. Dependencia de la plataforma.
7. Automatización de decisiones que afectan a ingresos, salud o vivienda.

**Certeza alta:** si el sistema toca trabajo, consumidores, pagos, salud, menores o datos sensibles, la regulación será estricta.  
**Certeza alta:** la confianza pública es un activo; un escándalo puede destruir la plataforma.

No todo debe autoactualizarse igual.

| Tipo de cambio | Automático | Condición | Aprobación |
|---|---:|---|---|

## 7.2 Fórmula de puntuación de hueco

Una fórmula práctica:

```text
Score_hueco =
  Dolor percibido
× Frecuencia
× Disposición a pagar
× Margen bruto estimado
× Facilidad de entrega
× Ventaja de datos
× Escalabilidad
÷ Riesgo legal
÷ Riesgo reputacional
÷ Intensidad competitiva
```

Traducción operativa:

| Variable | Métrica proxy |
|---|---|
| Dolor | Urgencia, quejas, tiempo de espera. |
| Frecuencia | Búsquedas/mes, solicitudes/mes. |
| Disposición a pagar | Precio medio de competencia, conversión. |
| Margen | Coste de proveedor + comisiones + soporte. |
| Facilidad | Estandarización, digitalización. |
| Ventaja de datos | Datos propios que mejoran matching. |
| Escalabilidad | Repetibilidad en otras ciudades. |
| Riesgo legal | Regulación, licencias, datos sensibles. |
| Riesgo reputacional | Daño potencial al cliente. |
| Competencia | Concentración, precios, calidad. |

## 7.3 Validación causal

No basta correlación. Para decidir si un hueco es negocio:

1. Landing page por servicio.
2. Tráfico pequeño pagado o local.
3. Medir conversión a solicitud.
4. Oferta manual con 3 proveedores.
5. Medir cierre, margen y satisfacción.
6. Si repite, automatizar.

Métodos: A/B, difference-in-differences, synthetic control, regresión discontinua si hay umbrales. Autores: Angrist y Pischke (2009); Imbens y Rubin (2015); Kohavi et al. (2020).

# 9. Motor de intermediación y comisión

## 9.1 Flujo de comisión

```text
Cliente solicita servicio
↓
Plataforma presupuesta
↓
Cliente paga 100 €
↓
Pago queda en escrow regulado
↓
Proveedor entrega
↓
Cliente confirma o disputa
↓
Plataforma libera:
  - 80–90 € al proveedor
  - 10–20 € comisión plataforma
  - menos fees de pago/IVA si aplica
```

## 9.2 Rangos de comisión orientativos

| Tipo | Comisión típica | Comentario |
|---|---:|---|
| Servicios locales estándar | 10–25 % | Si aportas cliente, cobro, seguro y garantía. |
| Servicios B2B recurrentes | 8–20 % | Menor take rate si ticket alto. |
| Productos digitales propios | 50–90 % margen | No hay proveedor externo o es bajo coste. |
| Lead generation | 5–30 € por lead o 10–30 % | Depende de calidad y exclusividad. |
| Suscripción proveedor | 19–199 €/mes | Herramientas, visibilidad, facturación. |
| Marketplace API | Revenue share | Para integradores y agentes externos. |

**Advertencia económica.** La comisión debe cubrir:

- Adquisición de cliente.
- Fraude y chargebacks.
- Soporte.
- Impuestos y facturación.
- Disputas.
- Seguros.
- Tecnología.
- Margen.

Si no cubre contribución positiva por transacción, escalar amplifica pérdidas.

**No confundas blindaje con opacidad.**

Opacidad = evasión, elusión agresiva, ocultación de titularidad, abuso de proveedores o usuarios. Eso aumenta riesgo penal, administrativo y reputacional.

# 14. Economía y KPIs

## 14.1 Fórmulas básicas

```text
GMV = valor bruto transaccionado
Net revenue = GMV × take rate
Margen bruto = net revenue − costes variables directos
CAC = gasto comercial / nuevos clientes
LTV ≈ ARPU × margen bruto / churn
Payback CAC = CAC / (ARPU × margen bruto mensual)
Contribución por pedido = net revenue − pago proveedor − fees − fraude − soporte variable
```

## 14.2 Objetivos orientativos

| Métrica | Objetivo inicial | Objetivo escala |
|---|---:|---:|
| Take rate servicios locales | 12–20 % | 15–25 % |
| Margen bruto digital | >60 % | >75 % |
| CAC payback | <6 meses | <3 meses |
| Match time local | <2 h | <15 min |
| Entrega a tiempo | >90 % | >97 % |
| CSAT | >4,5/5 | >4,7/5 |
| Fraude | <1 % | <0,3 % |
| Chargebacks | <0,7 % | <0,3 % |
| Automatización digital | 60 % STP | 85 % STP |
| Automatización físico | 20 % STP | 50 % STP |
| Retención 90 días | >30 % | >50 % |

**STP** = straight-through processing: transacción completada sin intervención humana.

# 20. Reflexión prospectiva

# 3. Triangulación del diseño

## 3.1 Perspectiva científica/económica

La empresa debe reducir costes de transacción, siguiendo a **Coase (1937)** y **Williamson (1985)**:

- Búsqueda.
- Confianza.
- Contrato.
- Pago.
- Cumplimiento.

Como plataforma de dos lados, debe resolver el problema del huevo y la gallina, según **Rochet y Tirole (2003)** y **Parker, Van Alstyne y Choudary (2016)**:

- Primero liquidez en un nicho.
- Luego efectos de red.
- Luego datos.
- Luego expansión.

La IA aporta automatización, pero no elimina la necesidad de gobierno. En sistemas multiagente, ver **Wooldridge (2009)** y **Russell & Norvig (2021)**. Para experimentación y optimización, ver **Kohavi, Tang y Xu (2020)**.

**Certeza alta:** sin liquidez inicial, la plataforma muere.  
**Certeza alta:** sin confianza, no hay comisión.  
**Certeza media:** la IA puede automatizar gran parte del backoffice.  
**Certeza baja:** autonomía total sin supervisión humana en servicios reales.

## 3.2 Perspectiva práctica

Para una sola persona, el sistema debe ser:

- No-code/low-code al inicio.
- Modular.
- Medible.
- Delegable a IA.
- Convertible en software propio después.

La secuencia correcta:

```text
Validar nicho
→ paquetizar servicio
→ conseguir 10 clientes
→ conseguir 5 proveedores
→ automatizar intake
→ automatizar pagos
→ automatizar seguimiento
→ automatizar producción digital
→ expandir categoría
→ expandir zona
```

## 3.3 Perspectiva ético-histórica

Las plataformas escalan rápido, pero pueden generar precarización, sesgos, dependencia y vigilancia. Ver **Zuboff (2019)**, **Scholz (2016)** y **Autor (2015)**.

Blindaje ético:

- Transparencia de comisiones.
- Derecho a apelación humana.
- No manipulación oscura.
- Minimización de datos.
- Proveedores tratados como socios.
- Calidad antes que crecimiento.

# 7. Modelo de datos completo

Esquema mínimo en PostgreSQL simplificado:

```sql
-- Usuarios y roles
CREATE TABLE users (
  id uuid PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  role text CHECK (role IN ('customer','provider','admin','ai_agent')),
  name text,
  phone text UNIQUE,
  email text,
  language text DEFAULT 'es',
  consent_marketing boolean DEFAULT false,
  consent_ai boolean DEFAULT false,
  created_at timestamptz DEFAULT now()
);

-- Proveedores
CREATE TABLE providers (
  id uuid PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  user_id uuid REFERENCES users(id),
  legal_name text,
  tax_id text,
  status text DEFAULT 'pending',
  kyc_status text DEFAULT 'pending',
  insurance_status text DEFAULT 'none',
  rating numeric DEFAULT 0,
  completed_jobs int DEFAULT 0,
  payout_account_id text,
  created_at timestamptz DEFAULT now()
);

-- Servicios/catálogo
CREATE TABLE services (
  id uuid PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  slug text UNIQUE,
  name text,
  category text,
  price_cents int,
  currency text DEFAULT 'EUR',
  commission_bps int, -- 2000 = 20%
  delivery_mode text CHECK (delivery_mode IN ('digital','onsite','hybrid')),
  risk_level text CHECK (risk_level IN ('low','medium','high')),
  active boolean DEFAULT true,
  created_at timestamptz DEFAULT now()
);

-- Leads
CREATE TABLE leads (
  id uuid PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  channel text,
  raw_message text,
  intent text,
  score int,
  status text DEFAULT 'new',
  user_id uuid REFERENCES users(id),
  service_id uuid REFERENCES services(id),
  created_at timestamptz DEFAULT now()
);

-- Pedidos
CREATE TABLE orders (
  id uuid PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  user_id uuid REFERENCES users(id),
  provider_id uuid REFERENCES providers(id),
  service_id uuid REFERENCES services(id),
  status text DEFAULT 'created',
  scheduled_at timestamptz,
  completed_at timestamptz,
  amount_cents int,
  commission_cents int,
  created_at timestamptz DEFAULT now()
);

-- Pagos
CREATE TABLE payments (
  id uuid PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  order_id uuid REFERENCES orders(id),
  stripe_payment_intent text,
  status text,
  amount_cents int,
  currency text DEFAULT 'EUR',
  created_at timestamptz DEFAULT now()
);

-- Reseñas
CREATE TABLE reviews (
  id uuid PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  order_id uuid REFERENCES orders(id),
  rating int CHECK (rating BETWEEN 1 AND 5),
  comment text,
  created_at timestamptz DEFAULT now()
);

-- Disputas
CREATE TABLE disputes (
  id uuid PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  order_id uuid REFERENCES orders(id),
  reason text,
  status text DEFAULT 'open',
  resolution text,
  refunded_cents int DEFAULT 0,
  created_at timestamptz DEFAULT now()
);

-- Eventos/auditoría
CREATE TABLE audit_events (
  id uuid PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  actor_type text,
  actor_id text,
  action text,
  entity text,
  entity_id text,
  metadata jsonb,
  created_at timestamptz DEFAULT now()
);

-- Consentimientos
CREATE TABLE consents (
  id uuid PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  user_id uuid REFERENCES users(id),
  consent_type text,
  granted boolean,
  version text,
  created_at timestamptz DEFAULT now()
);
```

# 15. SOPs esenciales

## SOP 1: Onboarding proveedor

```text
1. Proveedor rellena formulario.
2. IA verifica datos incompletos.
3. Humano entrevista 10 min.
4. Sube DNI/NIE y alta fiscal.
5. Sube seguro si aplica.
6. Firma términos y DPA.
7. Realiza prueba.
8. Activa con límite: primeros 3 pedidos supervisados.
9. Si rating >4,5 y 0 incidencias, pasa a estándar.
```

## SOP 2: Queja cliente

```text
1. IA clasifica gravedad.
2. Si grave, humano responde en <2 h.
3. Pedir evidencia.
4. Congelar pago si aplica.
5. Proponer solución:
   - repetir servicio
   - descuento
   - reembolso parcial
   - reembolso total
6. Registrar causa raíz.
7. Actualizar plantilla/protocolo.
```

## SOP 3: Fraude

```text
1. Risk Agent detecta score >70.
2. Orden pasa a hold.
3. Humano revisa.
4. Contactar cliente/proveedor.
5. Si fraude confirmado:
   - cancelar
   - reembolsar si procede
   - bloquear cuenta
   - log
   - reportar a autoridad si delito
```

## SOP 4: Solicitud RGPD

```text
1. Usuario solicita acceso/supresión.
2. Verificar identidad.
3. IA busca datos en sistemas.
4. Humano valida.
5. Responder en plazo legal.
6. Registrar consentimiento/resolución.
7. Borrar salvo obligación de conservación.
```

## SOP 5: Caída de sistema

```text
1. Alerta de monitoring.
2. Modo manual: formulario + email + teléfono.
3. Aviso a clientes afectados.
4. Registro de pedidos manuales.
5. Recuperación.
6. Informe postmortem.
```

def assess_risk(message: str) -> str:
    text = (message or "").lower()
    if any(keyword in text for keyword in HIGH_RISK_KEYWORDS):
        return "high"
    return "low"


def suggest_service_slug(
    message: str,
    services: list[Service],
    forced_slug: str | None = None,
) -> str | None:
    slugs = {service.slug for service in services}

    if forced_slug and forced_slug in slugs:
        return forced_slug

    text = (message or "").lower()

    for keywords, slug in SERVICE_RULES:
        if slug not in slugs:
            continue
        if any(keyword in text for keyword in keywords):
            return slug

    if services:
        return services[0].slug

    return None


def ai_intake_reply(
    message: str,
    services: list[Service],
    forced_slug: str | None = None,
) -> dict:
    risk = assess_risk(message)
    slug = suggest_service_slug(message, services, forced_slug)
    service = next((s for s in services if s.slug == slug), None)

    intent = slug or "general"
    score = 70 if service else 20

    if risk == "high":
        score = 95
        reply = (
            "Esto requiere atención humana. "
            "Lo paso al responsable. "
            "Por favor, deja un resumen breve y un teléfono de contacto."
        )
    elif service:
        score = 85
        reply = (
            f"Podemos ayudarte con: {service.name}. "
            f"Precio aproximado: {format_eur(service.price_cents)}. "
            f"Modalidad: {service.delivery_mode}. "
            "Si quieres, te envío link de pago y agenda disponible."
        )
    else:
        reply = (
            "Cuéntame un poco más: ¿qué necesitas, "
            "en qué zona y con qué urgencia?"
        )

    return {
        "reply": reply,
        "intent": intent,
        "suggested_service_slug": slug,
        "risk_level": risk,
        "score": score,
    }

def get_or_create_user_by_phone(
    db: Session,
    phone: str,
    name: str | None = None,
    email: str | None = None,
) -> User:
    user = db.query(User).filter(User.phone == phone).first()

    if user:
        changed = False
        if name and not user.name:
            user.name = name
            changed = True
        if email and not user.email:
            user.email = email
            changed = True
        if changed:
            db.commit()
            db.refresh(user)
        return user

    user = User(
        role="customer",
        phone=phone,
        name=name,
        email=email,
    )
    db.add(user)
    db.commit()
    db.refresh(user)

    log_event(
        db=db,
        action="user.created",
        entity="user",
        entity_id=user.id,
        metadata={"phone": phone},
    )

    return user

def assign_provider(db: Session, service: Service) -> Provider | None:
    if service.delivery_mode == "digital":
        return None

    provider = (
        db.query(Provider)
        .filter(Provider.status == "active")
        .order_by(
            Provider.rating_x100.desc(),
            Provider.completed_jobs.desc(),
        )
        .first()
    )

    return provider


def create_order(
    db: Session,
    user: User,
    service: Service,
    provider: Provider | None = None,
    scheduled_at: datetime | None = None,
) -> Order:
    commission_cents = int(service.price_cents * service.commission_bps / 10000)
    provider_payout_cents = service.price_cents - commission_cents

    order = Order(
        user_id=user.id,
        provider_id=provider.id if provider else None,
        service_id=service.id,
        status="created",
        scheduled_at=scheduled_at,
        amount_cents=service.price_cents,
        commission_cents=commission_cents,
        provider_payout_cents=provider_payout_cents,
        currency=service.currency,
    )

    db.add(order)
    db.commit()
    db.refresh(order)

    log_event(
        db=db,
        action="order.created",
        entity="order",
        entity_id=order.id,
        metadata={
            "service_slug": service.slug,
            "amount_cents": order.amount_cents,
            "commission_cents": order.commission_cents,
            "provider_id": order.provider_id,
        },
    )

    return order


def payment_succeeded(
    db: Session,
    order_id: str,
    payment_ref: str | None = None,
) -> Order:
    order = db.get(Order, order_id)
    if not order:
        raise HTTPException(status_code=404, detail="Order not found")

    if order.status in ["completed", "refunded", "cancelled"]:
        return order

    payment = (
        db.query(Payment)
        .filter(Payment.order_id == order_id)
        .order_by(Payment.created_at.desc())
        .first()
    )

    if payment:
        payment.status = "succeeded"
        payment.provider_payment_ref = payment_ref or payment.provider_payment_ref
    else:
        payment = Payment(
            order_id=order_id,
            provider_payment_ref=payment_ref,
            status="succeeded",
            amount_cents=order.amount_cents,
            currency=order.currency,
        )
        db.add(payment)

    if order.provider_id:
        order.status = "assigned"
    else:
        order.status = "paid"

    db.commit()
    db.refresh(order)

    log_event(
        db=db,
        action="payment.succeeded",
        entity="order",
        entity_id=order.id,
        metadata={
            "payment_ref": payment_ref,
            "amount_cents": order.amount_cents,
        },
    )

    return order

@app.post("/api/leads")
def create_lead(payload: LeadCreate, db: Session = Depends(get_db)):
    if not payload.phone and not payload.email:
        raise HTTPException(
            status_code=400,
            detail="Phone or email is required",
        )

    user = None

    if payload.phone:
        user = get_or_create_user_by_phone(
            db=db,
            phone=payload.phone,
            name=payload.name,
            email=payload.email,
        )
    else:
        user = User(
            role="customer",
            name=payload.name,
            email=payload.email,
        )
        db.add(user)
        db.commit()
        db.refresh(user)

    services = active_services(db)
    ai_reply = ai_intake_reply(
        message=payload.message,
        services=services,
        forced_slug=payload.service_slug,
    )

    service = None
    if ai_reply["suggested_service_slug"]:
        service = (
            db.query(Service)
            .filter(
                Service.slug == ai_reply["suggested_service_slug"],
                Service.active.is_(True),
            )
            .first()
        )

    lead = Lead(
        channel=payload.channel,
        user_id=user.id,
        service_id=service.id if service else None,
        raw_message=payload.message,
        intent=ai_reply["intent"],
        score=ai_reply["score"],
        status="new",
        risk_level=ai_reply["risk_level"],
    )

    db.add(lead)
    db.commit()
    db.refresh(lead)

    log_event(
        db=db,
        action="lead.created",
        entity="lead",
        entity_id=lead.id,
        metadata={
            "intent": lead.intent,
            "score": lead.score,
            "risk_level": lead.risk_level,
        },
    )

    return {
        "lead": jsonable_encoder(lead),
        "ai_reply": ai_reply,
    }

        )

    order = create_order(
        db=db,
        user=user,
        service=service,
        provider=provider,
        scheduled_at=payload.scheduled_at,
    )

@app.post("/api/orders/{order_id}/pay")
def pay_order(order_id: str, db: Session = Depends(get_db)):
    order = db.get(Order, order_id)
    if not order:
        raise HTTPException(status_code=404, detail="Order not found")

    if order.status in ["completed", "refunded", "cancelled"]:
        raise HTTPException(status_code=400, detail="Order cannot be paid")

    succeeded_payment = (
        db.query(Payment)
        .filter(
            Payment.order_id == order.id,
            Payment.status == "succeeded",
        )
        .first()
    )

    if succeeded_payment:
        return jsonable_encoder(succeeded_payment)

    pending_payment = (
        db.query(Payment)
        .filter(
            Payment.order_id == order.id,
            Payment.status == "pending",
        )
        .first()
    )

    if pending_payment:
        return jsonable_encoder(pending_payment)

    payment_ref = f"pi_mock_{order.id}"

    payment = Payment(
        order_id=order.id,
        provider_payment_ref=payment_ref,
        status="pending",
        amount_cents=order.amount_cents,
        currency=order.currency,
    )

    db.add(payment)
    db.commit()
    db.refresh(payment)

    log_event(
        db=db,
        action="payment.created",
        entity="payment",
        entity_id=payment.id,
        metadata={
            "order_id": order.id,
            "amount_cents": payment.amount_cents,
        },
    )

    return {
        "payment": jsonable_encoder(payment),
        "client_secret": f"{payment_ref}_secret",
        "mock_confirm_url": f"/api/payments/{payment.id}/mock_success",
        "note": "En producción, usar Stripe PaymentIntent real y webhook verificado.",
    }

@app.post("/webhooks/stripe")
def stripe_webhook(event: StripeWebhookEvent, db: Session = Depends(get_db)):
    """
    Webhook simplificado para demostración.

    En producción:
    - Verificar firma Stripe.
    - Usar STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET.
    - Idempotencia.
    - Reintentos.
    """
    if event.type != "payment_intent.succeeded":
        return {"received": True}

    obj = event.data.get("object", {})
    payment_ref = obj.get("id")
    metadata = obj.get("metadata", {})
    order_id = metadata.get("order_id")

    payment = None

    if payment_ref:
        payment = (
            db.query(Payment)
            .filter(Payment.provider_payment_ref == payment_ref)
            .first()
        )

    if not payment and order_id:
        payment = (
            db.query(Payment)
            .filter(Payment.order_id == order_id)
            .first()
        )

    if not payment:
        return {
            "received": True,
            "warning": "Payment not found",
        }

    order = payment_succeeded(
        db=db,
        order_id=payment.order_id,
        payment_ref=payment_ref or payment.provider_payment_ref,
    )

    return {
        "received": True,
        "order_id": order.id,
    }

@app.post("/api/reviews")
def create_review(payload: ReviewCreate, db: Session = Depends(get_db)):
    order = db.get(Order, payload.order_id)
    if not order:
        raise HTTPException(status_code=404, detail="Order not found")

    if order.status != "completed":
        raise HTTPException(
            status_code=400,
            detail="Only completed orders can be reviewed",
        )

    existing = (
        db.query(Review)
        .filter(Review.order_id == order.id)
        .first()
    )

    if existing:
        raise HTTPException(status_code=400, detail="Review already exists")

    review = Review(
        order_id=order.id,
        rating=payload.rating,
        comment=payload.comment,
    )

    db.add(review)

    if order.provider_id:
        provider = db.get(Provider, order.provider_id)
        if provider:
            count = max(provider.completed_jobs or 1, 1)
            old_sum = (provider.rating_x100 or 0) * (count - 1)
            new_sum = old_sum + payload.rating * 100
            provider.rating_x100 = int(new_sum / count)

    db.commit()
    db.refresh(review)

    log_event(
        db=db,
        action="review.created",
        entity="review",
        entity_id=review.id,
        metadata={
            "order_id": order.id,
            "rating": payload.rating,
        },
    )

    return jsonable_encoder(review)

@app.post("/api/disputes/{dispute_id}/resolve", dependencies=[Depends(require_admin)])
def resolve_dispute(dispute_id: str, payload: DisputeResolve, db: Session = Depends(get_db)):
    dispute = db.get(Dispute, dispute_id)
    if not dispute:
        raise HTTPException(status_code=404, detail="Dispute not found")

    if dispute.status != "open":
        raise HTTPException(status_code=400, detail="Dispute already resolved")

    order = db.get(Order, dispute.order_id)
    if not order:
        raise HTTPException(status_code=404, detail="Order not found")

    refund_cents = min(payload.refunded_cents, order.amount_cents)

    dispute.status = "resolved"
    dispute.resolution = payload.resolution
    dispute.refunded_cents = refund_cents

    if refund_cents >= order.amount_cents:
        order.status = "refunded"
    else:
        order.status = "completed"

    if refund_cents > 0:
        payment = (
            db.query(Payment)
            .filter(
                Payment.order_id == order.id,
                Payment.status == "succeeded",
            )
            .order_by(Payment.created_at.desc())
            .first()
        )
        if payment:
            payment.status = "refunded"

    db.commit()
    db.refresh(dispute)

    log_event(
        db=db,
        action="dispute.resolved",
        entity="dispute",
        entity_id=dispute.id,
        actor_type="admin",
        metadata={
            "order_id": order.id,
            "refunded_cents": refund_cents,
        },
    )

    return jsonable_encoder(dispute)

@app.get("/admin/metrics", dependencies=[Depends(require_admin)])
def admin_metrics(db: Session = Depends(get_db)):
    leads_count = db.scalar(select(func.count(Lead.id))) or 0
    orders_count = db.scalar(select(func.count(Order.id))) or 0
    users_count = db.scalar(select(func.count(User.id))) or 0
    providers_active = db.scalar(
        select(func.count(Provider.id)).where(Provider.status == "active")
    ) or 0
    services_active = db.scalar(
        select(func.count(Service.id)).where(Service.active.is_(True))
    ) or 0
    disputes_open = db.scalar(
        select(func.count(Dispute.id)).where(Dispute.status == "open")
    ) or 0

    paid_statuses = ["paid", "assigned", "completed"]

    gmv_cents = db.scalar(
        select(func.coalesce(func.sum(Order.amount_cents), 0)).where(
            Order.status.in_(paid_statuses)
        )
    ) or 0

    commission_cents = db.scalar(
        select(func.coalesce(func.sum(Order.commission_cents), 0)).where(
            Order.status.in_(paid_statuses)
        )
    ) or 0

    completed_orders = db.scalar(
        select(func.count(Order.id)).where(Order.status == "completed")
    ) or 0

    return {
        "users": users_count,
        "leads": leads_count,
        "orders": orders_count,
        "completed_orders": completed_orders,
        "providers_active": providers_active,
        "services_active": services_active,
        "disputes_open": disputes_open,
        "gmv_cents": gmv_cents,
        "commission_cents": commission_cents,
        "gmv_eur": round(gmv_cents / 100, 2),
        "commission_eur": round(commission_cents / 100, 2),
    }

class InventionEngine:
    async def generate_solutions(self, opportunity: dict):
        """Generar n=100 arquitecturas empresariales diferentes"""
        solutions = []
        
        for i in range(100):
            # Variar: modelo de negocio, positioning, target, channels, tech stack
            solution = {
                "id": uuid4(),
                "opportunity_id": opportunity["id"],
                "business_model": random_pick(BUSINESS_MODELS),  # SaaS, Marketplace, Agency, etc.
                "target_segment": random_pick(SEGMENTS),  # SME, Enterprise, Individuals, etc.
                "channels": random_pick_multiple(CHANNELS),  # Web, Mobile, WhatsApp, APIs, etc.
                "pricing_strategy": random_pick(PRICING),  # Subscription, Per-transaction, Freemium, etc.
                "tech_stack": random_pick(STACKS),  # FastAPI+React, Django+Vue, etc.
                "initial_investment_euros": random.uniform(1000, 50000),
                "break_even_months": random.uniform(2, 24),
                "margin_target_percent": random.uniform(20, 80),
            }
            
            # Simular viabilidad
            simulation = await self.monte_carlo_simulation(solution, opportunity, n=10000)
            solution["viability_score"] = simulation["success_probability"]
            solution["roi_expected"] = simulation["expected_roi"]
            solution["risk_profile"] = simulation["drawdown_max"]
            
            solutions.append(solution)
        
        # Seleccionar: top 3 por viabilidad, top 3 por ROI, top 3 por velocidad de lanzamiento
        return {
            "by_viability": sorted(solutions, key=lambda x: x["viability_score"], reverse=True)[:3],
            "by_roi": sorted(solutions, key=lambda x: x["roi_expected"], reverse=True)[:3],
            "by_speed": sorted(solutions, key=lambda x: x["break_even_months"])[:3],
            "all_candidates": solutions  # Para auditoría
        }
    
    async def monte_carlo_simulation(self, solution, opportunity, n=10000):
        """Simular 10,000 ejecuciones del plan"""
        outcomes = []
        
        for simulation_id in range(n):
            # Parámetros estocásticos
            market_penetration = random.gauss(0.05, 0.02)  # Media 5%, sigma 2%
            churn_rate = random.gauss(0.03, 0.01)
            cac = random.gauss(solution["initial_investment_euros"] / 100, 10)
            ltv = cac * (1 / churn_rate) if churn_rate > 0 else cac * 12
            
            # Simulación temporal (24 meses)
            cash_flow = simulate_24_months(
                initial_cash=solution["initial_investment_euros"],
                monthly_users=opportunity["tam"] * market_penetration / 24,
                cac=cac,
                ltv=ltv,
                churn=churn_rate,
                burn_rate=solution["initial_investment_euros"] / 12
            )
            
            # Outcome
            final_cash = sum(cash_flow)
            roi = final_cash / solution["initial_investment_euros"] if final_cash > 0 else 0
            
            outcomes.append({
                "roi": roi,
                "final_cash": final_cash,
                "drawdown": min(cash_flow),
                "months_to_breakeven": next((i for i, cf in enumerate(cash_flow) if cf > 0), None)
            })
        
        return {
            "success_probability": len([o for o in outcomes if o["roi"] > 1]) / n,
            "expected_roi": mean([o["roi"] for o in outcomes]),
            "roi_percentile_10": percentile([o["roi"] for o in outcomes], 0.1),
            "roi_percentile_90": percentile([o["roi"] for o in outcomes], 0.9),
            "drawdown_max": min([o["drawdown"] for o in outcomes]),
            "median_breakeven_months": median([o["months_to_breakeven"] for o in outcomes if o["months_to_breakeven"]])
        }
Output: Ranking científico de soluciones por viabilidad + simulaciones auditables.

        roi = (total_revenue - total_spend) / total_spend if total_spend > 0 else 0

            "market": await self.market_intel.assess(proposal),  # ¿Hay demanda real?
            "tech": await self.tech_intel.assess(proposal),      # ¿Es viable técnicamente?
            "legal": await self.legal_intel.assess(proposal),    # ¿Es legal?

            "ethics": await self.ethics_intel.assess(proposal),  # ¿Es ético?

            "comply": await self.comply_intel.assess(proposal)   # ¿Cumple reglamentaciones?
        }

        conflicts = self.find_contradictions(opinions)
        if conflicts:

        approval_rate = sum(votes) / len(votes)

class AutonomousEconomy:
    def __init__(self, capital_euros: int):
        self.total_capital = capital_euros
        self.cash_available = capital_euros
        self.cash_deployed = {}  # business_id → euros
        self.cash_reserved = {}   # risk mitigation pools
        self.revenue_streams = {}  # business_id → cumulative revenue
    
    async def allocate_capital(self, businesses_pending_approval: list):
        """Decidir cuánto capital asignar a cada negocio"""
        
        for business in businesses_pending_approval:
            # 1. Calcular risk-adjusted capital allocation
            required_capital = business.initial_investment_euros
            roi_expected = business.roi_expected
            risk_score = business.risk_profile
            
            # Kelly Criterion para inversión óptima
            kelly_fraction = (roi_expected * 0.85 - (1 - 0.85)) / roi_expected if roi_expected > 1 else 0.1
            kelly_fraction = max(0.05, min(0.3, kelly_fraction))  # Limitar entre 5% y 30% del capital
            
            allocated_capital = int(self.total_capital * kelly_fraction)
            
            # 2. Verificar si hay capital disponible
            if allocated_capital > self.cash_available:
                allocated_capital = int(self.cash_available * 0.1)  # Asignar 10% de lo disponible
            
            # 3. Reservar capital para riesgos (10% adicional)
            reserved = int(allocated_capital * 0.1)
            
            # 4. Registrar
            self.cash_deployed[business.id] = allocated_capital
            self.cash_reserved[business.id] = reserved
            self.cash_available -= (allocated_capital + reserved)
            
            logger.info(f"Capital assigned to {business.name}: {allocated_capital}€ (risk: {risk_score})")
    
    async def manage_revenue_streams(self):
        """Recolectar ingresos, pagar deudas, reinvertir"""
        
        for business_id, revenue in self.revenue_streams.items():
            # 1. Pagar costes de operación
            opex = self.get_opex(business_id)
            net_revenue = revenue - opex
            
            # 2. Calcular impuestos (20% simplificado)
            taxes = int(net_revenue * 0.2)
            available_for_deployment = net_revenue - taxes
            
            # 3. Decidir: reinvertir o acumular
            if self.should_reinvest(business_id):
                # Reinvertir 70% en expansion
                self.cash_available += int(available_for_deployment * 0.7)
                # Guardar 30% como reserva
                self.reserves += int(available_for_deployment * 0.3)
            else:
                # Si el negocio es estable, guardar todo
                self.reserves += available_for_deployment
    
    async def rebalance_portfolio(self):
        """Cada semana: revisar si capital está bien asignado"""
        
        for business_id, capital_deployed in self.cash_deployed.items():
            roi_actual = self.calculate_roi(business_id)
            roi_target = self.businesses[business_id].roi_target
            
            # Si ROI cae 30% por debajo del target, reducir capital
            if roi_actual < roi_target * 0.7:
                await self.governance_council.debate_and_vote({
                    "action": "REDUCE_CAPITAL",
                    "business_id": business_id,
                    "reason": f"ROI {roi_actual} < target {roi_target}"
                })

"""
AUREA X — Universal Memory
Registro inmutable tipo hash-chain. Todo evento queda auditado.
Sin memoria no hay aprendizaje. Sin aprendizaje no hay evolución.
"""

import hashlib
import json
import sqlite3
import threading
from datetime import datetime, timezone
from pathlib import Path
from typing import Any


class UniversalMemory:
    """
    Append-only ledger con hash chain.
    Cada evento referencia el hash anterior → cadena de custodia.
    Almacenamiento: SQLite local (MVP) → PostgreSQL (producción).
    """

    def __init__(self, db_path: str = "aurea_x_memory.db"):
        self.db_path = db_path
        self._lock = threading.Lock()
        self._last_hash: str = "GENESIS"
        self._init_db()
        self._recover_last_hash()

    def _init_db(self):
        with sqlite3.connect(self.db_path) as conn:
            conn.execute("""
                CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS events (
                    seq INTEGER PRIMARY KEY AUTOINCREMENT,
                    event_type TEXT NOT NULL,
                    payload TEXT NOT NULL,
                    previous_hash TEXT NOT NULL,
                    event_hash TEXT NOT NULL,
                    created_at TEXT NOT NULL
                )
            """)
            conn.execute("""
                CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS idx_events_type
                ON events(event_type)
            """)
            conn.execute("""
                CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS idx_events_created
                ON events(created_at)
            """)
            conn.commit()

    def _recover_last_hash(self):
        with sqlite3.connect(self.db_path) as conn:
            row = conn.execute(
                "SELECT event_hash FROM events ORDER BY seq DESC LIMIT 1"
            ).fetchone()
            if row:
                self._last_hash = row[0]

    @staticmethod
    def _compute_hash(payload: dict, previous_hash: str) -> str:
        canonical = json.dumps(payload, sort_keys=True, default=str)
        raw = f"{previous_hash}|{canonical}"
        return hashlib.sha256(raw.encode()).hexdigest()

    def log(self, event_type: str, payload: dict[str, Any]) -> str:
        with self._lock:
            now = datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat()
            event_hash = self._compute_hash(payload, self._last_hash)

            with sqlite3.connect(self.db_path) as conn:
                conn.execute(
                    "INSERT INTO events (event_type, payload, previous_hash, event_hash, created_at) VALUES (?, ?, ?, ?, ?)",
                    (
                        event_type,
                        json.dumps(payload, default=str),
                        self._last_hash,
                        event_hash,
                        now,
                    ),
                )
                conn.commit()

            self._last_hash = event_hash
            return event_hash

    def query(
        self,
        event_type: str | None = None,
        since: str | None = None,
        limit: int = 100,
    ) -> list[dict]:
        clauses = []
        params: list = []

        if event_type:
            clauses.append("event_type = ?")
            params.append(event_type)
        if since:
            clauses.append("created_at >= ?")
            params.append(since)

        where = f"WHERE {' AND '.join(clauses)}" if clauses else ""
        sql = f"SELECT seq, event_type, payload, event_hash, created_at FROM events {where} ORDER BY seq DESC LIMIT ?"
        params.append(limit)

        with sqlite3.connect(self.db_path) as conn:
            rows = conn.execute(sql, params).fetchall()

        return [
            {
                "seq": r[0],
                "event_type": r[1],
                "payload": json.loads(r[2]),
                "hash": r[3],
                "created_at": r[4],
            }
            for r in rows
        ]

    def verify_chain(self) -> bool:
        with sqlite3.connect(self.db_path) as conn:
            rows = conn.execute(
                "SELECT payload, previous_hash, event_hash FROM events ORDER BY seq ASC"
            ).fetchall()

        prev = "GENESIS"
        for payload_str, stored_prev, stored_hash in rows:
            if stored_prev != prev:
                return False
            expected = self._compute_hash(json.loads(payload_str), stored_prev)
            if expected != stored_hash:
                return False
            prev = stored_hash
        return True

    def stats(self) -> dict:
        with sqlite3.connect(self.db_path) as conn:
            total = conn.execute("SELECT COUNT(*) FROM events").fetchone()[0]
            by_type = conn.execute(
                "SELECT event_type, COUNT(*) FROM events GROUP BY event_type ORDER BY COUNT(*) DESC"
            ).fetchall()
        return {
            "total_events": total,
            "by_type": {t: c for t, c in by_type},
            "chain_valid": self.verify_chain(),
        }

            cac_spend = conversions * max(1.0, random.gauss(cfg.cac_eur, cfg.cac_eur * 0.3))

            disputes = 0.0
            if conversions > 0 and random.random() < cfg.dispute_rate:
                disputes = conversions * ticket * cfg.refund_rate * commission_rate

            net = revenue - opex - cac_spend - disputes
            cash += net
            cumulative_net += net

            if cash > peak_cash:
                peak_cash = cash
            drawdown = peak_cash - cash
            if drawdown > max_drawdown:
                max_drawdown = drawdown

            if breakeven_day is None and cumulative_net > 0:
                breakeven_day = day

            daily_results.append(
                DayResult(
                    day=day,
                    leads=leads,
                    conversions=conversions,
                    revenue_eur=round(revenue, 2),
                    opex_eur=round(opex + cac_spend, 2),
                    disputes_eur=round(disputes, 2),
                    net_eur=round(net, 2),
                    cumulative_net_eur=round(cumulative_net, 2),
                )

        total_opex = sum(d.opex_eur for d in daily_results)
        total_disputes = sum(d.disputes_eur for d in daily_results)
        net_profit = total_revenue - total_opex - total_disputes
        roi = (net_profit / cfg.initial_capital_eur) if cfg.initial_capital_eur > 0 else 0.0

        return SimulationOutcome(
            run_id=run_id,
            total_revenue_eur=round(total_revenue, 2),
            total_opex_eur=round(total_opex, 2),
            total_disputes_eur=round(total_disputes, 2),
            net_profit_eur=round(net_profit, 2),

        passing = [o for o in outcomes if o.roi >= cfg.roi_target]
        success_prob = len(passing) / len(outcomes) if outcomes else 0.0

        rois_sorted = sorted(rois)
        n = len(rois_sorted)
        p10 = rois_sorted[int(n * 0.10)] if n > 0 else 0.0
        p50 = statistics.median(rois_sorted) if n > 0 else 0.0
        p90 = rois_sorted[int(n * 0.90)] if n > 0 else 0.0

        dd_sorted = sorted(drawdowns, reverse=True)
        dd_p95 = dd_sorted[int(len(dd_sorted) * 0.05)] if dd_sorted else 0.0

        recommendations: list[str] = []

            status = "PASS"

            recommendations.append("Lanzar con presupuesto reducido (50%) y revisión en día 30")
        else:
            status = "FAIL"

            p10_roi=round(p10, 4),
            p90_roi=round(p90, 4),
            median_breakeven_days=int(statistics.median(breakevens)) if breakevens else None,
            max_drawdown_p95_eur=round(dd_p95, 2),
            total_runs=len(outcomes),
            passing_runs=len(passing),
            failure_modes=failure_modes,
            recommendations=recommendations,
            generated_at=datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat(),
        )

"""
AUREA X — Evolutionary System
Auto-mejora de todos los componentes con evaluación rigurosa.
Ningún cambio entra en producción sin superar evals + shadow + canary.

Fundamento:
- Experimentación: Kohavi, Tang & Xu (2020), Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments.
- Continuous Delivery: Humble & Farley (2010).
- Evals de IA: OpenAI Evals framework (2023).
- Bandits: Sutton & Barto (2018), Reinforcement Learning, cap. 2.

import random
import statistics
from dataclasses import dataclass, field
from datetime import datetime, timezone
from enum import Enum


class ComponentStatus(Enum):
    STABLE = "stable"
    CANDIDATE = "candidate"
    SHADOW = "shadow"
    CANARY = "canary"
    PROMOTED = "promoted"
    ROLLED_BACK = "rolled_back"


@dataclass
class ComponentVersion:
    component_name: str
    version: int
    config: dict
    status: ComponentStatus = ComponentStatus.STABLE
    eval_score: float = 0.0
    created_at: str = field(default_factory=lambda: datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat())
    promoted_at: str | None = None
    rolled_back_at: str | None = None


@dataclass
class EvalResult:
    component_name: str
    version_a: int
    version_b: int
    score_a: float
    score_b: float
    improvement_pct: float
    sample_size: int
    statistically_significant: bool
    verdict: str
    evaluated_at: str


@dataclass
class EvolutionEvent:
    component_name: str
    from_version: int
    to_version: int
    trigger: str
    improvement_pct: float
    approved_by: str
    timestamp: str


class EvolutionarySystem:

    MIN_IMPROVEMENT_PCT = 2.0
    MIN_SAMPLE_SIZE = 100
    CANARY_TRAFFIC_PCT = 5.0
    CANARY_DURATION_HOURS = 48

    def __init__(self, memory=None):
        self.memory = memory
        self.registry: dict[str, list[ComponentVersion]] = {}
        self.evolution_log: list[EvolutionEvent] = []

    def register_component(self, name: str, version: int, config: dict):
        cv = ComponentVersion(
            component_name=name,
            version=version,
            config=config,
            status=ComponentStatus.STABLE,
        )
        if name not in self.registry:
            self.registry[name] = []
        self.registry[name].append(cv)

    def get_active_version(self, name: str) -> ComponentVersion | None:
        versions = self.registry.get(name, [])
        for v in reversed(versions):
            if v.status in (ComponentStatus.STABLE, ComponentStatus.PROMOTED):
                return v
        return versions[-1] if versions else None

    def propose_candidate(self, name: str, new_config: dict) -> ComponentVersion | None:
        versions = self.registry.get(name, [])
        if not versions:
            return None

        current = self.get_active_version(name)
        if not current:
            return None

        new_version = current.version + 1
        candidate = ComponentVersion(
            component_name=name,
            version=new_version,
            config=new_config,
            status=ComponentStatus.CANDIDATE,
        )
        versions.append(candidate)

        if self.memory:
            self.memory.log(
                "evolution_candidate_proposed",
                {
                    "component": name,
                    "version": new_version,
                    "config_diff": self._diff_configs(current.config, new_config),
                },
            )

        return candidate

    @staticmethod
    def _diff_configs(a: dict, b: dict) -> dict:
        diff = {}
        all_keys = set(list(a.keys()) + list(b.keys()))
        for k in all_keys:
            va = a.get(k)
            vb = b.get(k)
            if va != vb:
                diff[k] = {"from": va, "to": vb}
        return diff

    def run_eval(
        self,
        name: str,
        version_a: int,
        version_b: int,
        golden_set_size: int = 200,
    ) -> EvalResult:
        versions = {v.version: v for v in self.registry.get(name, [])}
        va = versions.get(version_a)
        vb = versions.get(version_b)

        if not va or not vb:
            raise ValueError(f"Version not found for {name}")

        score_a = self._simulate_eval_score(va.config, golden_set_size)
        score_b = self._simulate_eval_score(vb.config, golden_set_size)

        improvement = ((score_b - score_a) / max(score_a, 0.001)) * 100
        significant = abs(improvement) > self.MIN_IMPROVEMENT_PCT and golden_set_size >= self.MIN_SAMPLE_SIZE

        if significant and improvement > 0:
            verdict = "PROMOTE"
        elif significant and improvement < 0:
            verdict = "REJECT"
        else:
            verdict = "INCONCLUSIVE"

        result = EvalResult(
            component_name=name,
            version_a=version_a,
            version_b=version_b,
            score_a=round(score_a, 4),
            score_b=round(score_b, 4),
            improvement_pct=round(improvement, 2),
            sample_size=golden_set_size,
            statistically_significant=significant,
            verdict=verdict,
            evaluated_at=datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat(),
        )

        if self.memory:
            self.memory.log("evolution_eval", result.__dict__)

        return result

    @staticmethod
    def _simulate_eval_score(config: dict, n: int) -> float:
        base = config.get("quality_weight", 0.7)
        noise = random.gauss(0, 0.03)
        scores = [min(1.0, max(0.0, base + random.gauss(0, 0.1) + noise)) for _ in range(n)]
        return statistics.mean(scores)

    def promote(self, name: str, version: int, approved_by: str = "governance_council"):
        versions = self.registry.get(name, [])
        for v in versions:
            if v.version == version:
                v.status = ComponentStatus.PROMOTED
                v.promoted_at = datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat()

                for other in versions:
                    if other.version != version and other.status in (
                        ComponentStatus.STABLE,
                        ComponentStatus.PROMOTED,
                    ):
                        other.status = ComponentStatus.ROLLED_BACK
                        other.rolled_back_at = datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat()

                if self.memory:
                    self.memory.log(
                        "evolution_promoted",
                        {"component": name, "version": version, "approved_by": approved_by},
                    )
                return

        raise ValueError(f"Version {version} not found for {name}")

    def rollback(self, name: str, version: int, reason: str):
        versions = self.registry.get(name, [])
        for v in versions:
            if v.version == version:
                v.status = ComponentStatus.ROLLED_BACK
                v.rolled_back_at = datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat()

                previous = [
                    pv for pv in versions
                    if pv.version < version and pv.status != ComponentStatus.ROLLED_BACK
                ]
                if previous:
                    prev = previous[-1]
                    prev.status = ComponentStatus.STABLE

                if self.memory:
                    self.memory.log(
                        "evolution_rollback",
                        {"component": name, "version": version, "reason": reason},
                    )
                return

        raise ValueError(f"Version {version} not found for {name}")

    def run_evolution_cycle(self, component_configs: dict[str, dict]):
        for name, new_config in component_configs.items():
            current = self.get_active_version(name)
            if not current:
                self.register_component(name, 1, new_config)
                continue

            candidate = self.propose_candidate(name, new_config)
            if not candidate:
                continue

            eval_result = self.run_eval(name, current.version, candidate.version)

            if eval_result.verdict == "PROMOTE":
                self.promote(name, candidate.version)
                self.evolution_log.append(
                    EvolutionEvent(
                        component_name=name,
                        from_version=current.version,
                        to_version=candidate.version,
                        trigger="eval_improvement",
                        improvement_pct=eval_result.improvement_pct,
                        approved_by="evolutionary_system",
                        timestamp=datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat(),
                    )
                )
            elif eval_result.verdict == "REJECT":
                self.rollback(name, candidate.version, "eval_regression")
            else:
                candidate.status = ComponentStatus.ROLLED_BACK

    def report(self) -> dict:
        active = {}
        for name, versions in self.registry.items():
            current = self.get_active_version(name)
            if current:
                active[name] = {
                    "version": current.version,
                    "status": current.status.value,
                    "eval_score": current.eval_score,
                }

        return {
            "active_components": active,
            "total_evolution_events": len(self.evolution_log),
            "recent_events": [
                {
                    "component": e.component_name,
                    "from": e.from_version,
                    "to": e.to_version,
                    "improvement_pct": e.improvement_pct,
                    "timestamp": e.timestamp,
                }
                for e in self.evolution_log[-10:]
            ],
        }

        report["phases"]["evolution"] = evolution_report

        memory_stats = self.memory.stats()
        report["phases"]["memory"] = memory_stats

        report["cycle_end"] = datetime.now(timezone.utc).isoformat()

        self.memory.log("weekly_cycle_completed", report)

        with open("aurea_x_weekly_report_v2.json", "w", encoding="utf-8") as f:

        return {
            "total_capital_eur": total_capital,

## Reflexión prospectiva

AUREA X ya tiene los 10 componentes estructurales. Pero el sistema es tan bueno como sus datos de entrada y sus límites de gobernanza. Un Discovery Engine sin APIs reales produce oportunidades ficticias. Un Digital Twin sin datos históricos reales simula sobre supuestos. Un Evolutionary System sin golden sets reales optimiza ruido.

La próxima frontera no es más código. Es:

1. **Conectar APIs reales** (Google Trends, Reddit, GitHub, BOE, EUR-Lex).
2. **Alimentar el Digital Twin con datos reales** del piloto en Pubilla Cases.
3. **Construir golden sets de evaluación** con transacciones reales.
4. **Validar la gobernanza** con casos límite reales (disputas, fraudes, regulación).
